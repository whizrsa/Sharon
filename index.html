Letters From Sharon

The first time she said it

On our movie date, I wanted to tell you something important — something I've been wanting to say for a long time — but I couldn't find the courage in that moment. In place of that, I asked if I could kiss you because I thought through my kiss I could convey my deep feelings.

"I love you, Elvis. I think I have for much longer than I ever admitted to myself."

Back in high school, I was afraid of the unknown, even though deep down I always knew I wanted you. I sabotaged something beautiful because I didn't feel good enough at the time. But I thank God for giving me a second chance and bringing us back to each other.

I kept your love letter because a part of me always hoped our paths would cross again.

I love you for being you — for being a steady pillar in my life all these years, first as a friend I didn't know I needed, and now as my partner. I love your kindness, your honesty, your sincerity.

Lately, all I dream and pray about is a good future for us — a future where we grow together, build together, and choose each other every day. I want you in my life as my forever partner, and I can't wait for the day that becomes our reality.

I really do love you, Elvis. I thought I could wait until I had the courage to say it face to face, but my heart is too full to keep it in any longer.

— Sharon

Her promise to me

You're just the sweetest. Sometimes I ask myself what I've done in my previous life to be this blessed and lucky to have you in my life.

"I love you more than words could convey and I want to spend the rest of my life with you."

I only see a future with you in it and no one else, and I wouldn't have it any other way. You understand me and love me in ways I never thought possible. You bring a sense of peace and calmness with you, and I feel the safest in your presence.

Just so you know — I'm here to stay, to love you and to be loved by you. I'm your wife and you're my husband. I can't wait for the whole world to officially know that I only belong to you and you belong to me only.

I can't wait to wake up next to you, for you to make love to me and only me day and night, to only be claimed by you and receive all those amazing hugs and kisses from you.

I can't wait to officially become one with you, because I know and feel that God has definitely blessed this union.

I love you so much.

— Sharon, forever yours
